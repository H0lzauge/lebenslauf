## 2001 – 2005 
### Freelancer

After graduating, I started out working as a freelancer. I did this almost exclusively for GIABmbH in Erfurt, Germany. My work primarily involved evaluating economic surveys and analyzing other economic data. 

I took a break from this job for a little over six months to complete an internship at FER Mexicana, a supplier in the automotive industry based in Puebla, Mexico. My responsibilities were as varied as is typical in the management of a small company.
