## 2018 – 2022 
### Data Officer of Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (FOCBS)

As the first and sole Data Officer, I built and established the BAZG’s data governance organization. It is based on the data policy I developed. Serving as an interface between IT and management, the data governance organization is a central component of the FOCBS’s digital transformation process.
Specifically, this task involved implementing a software-based system for organizing data, including the assignment of domains, ownership, and responsibilities; above all, however, it involved defining processes. 

As part of the large-scale DaziT project, my role was to work with my team to develop and maintain a comprehensive data model. My team consisted primarily of business analysts. I was also responsible for ensuring data quality at both the procedural and technical levels. In addition, I represented the FOCBS in the development of e-government solutions on federal level.
