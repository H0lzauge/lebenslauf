## 2011 – 2018 
### Statistician and data scientist Post CH AG

During my first year at the Post Office, I worked in market research. I conducted customer satisfaction surveys and focused in particular on analyzing customer journeys. 

I then moved to the Finance department, where I held a management position. In addition to my primary responsibility of analyzing financial and inventory flows, I worked with the business unit to develop ideas for projects, identify appropriate methods and processes, and then implement them in production in collaboration with IT. I also won an internal innovation award for this work. The projects spanned a wide range of departments, including logistics, finance, and controlling. One example was forecasting the arrival times of package shipments.
